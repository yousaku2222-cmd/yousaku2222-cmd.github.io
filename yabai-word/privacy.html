Yabai Word - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026 / Last updated: October 10, 2026

"Yabai Word" (the "App") is provided by an independent developer, Yusaku Mizogami ("the Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles.

1. Information the App collects and stores

1.1 Stored only on your device

The App does not require an account. The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Developer:

  • Your favorited words and archive progress
  • Quiz history and your daily free-quiz usage status
  • Your notification time preference and display language (English or Japanese)
  • Your premium subscription status (used to unlock features locally)

Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device (in-app purchase records are tied to your Apple ID or Google account and can be restored).

1.2 Content delivery (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) to download the slang word database. No personal or account information is sent to Cloud Firestore.

The App also downloads in-app notices (update announcements) from the Developer's website. This is a plain download; nothing is sent from your device.

1.3 Usage analytics (Firebase Analytics)

From version 1.1.0, the App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how its features are used, so that the Developer can decide what to improve. Besides the standard events Firebase Analytics collects automatically (such as app opens, sessions and screen views), the App records:

  • Which display language you use (English or Japanese)
  • When you start, finish or share the word-age check, and the resulting age and title
  • When the Premium screen is opened and from which screen
  • When a subscription purchase is started or completed, and which plan

Firebase Analytics also uses an app-instance identifier and basic device information (such as OS version and country). This data is not linked to your name or any account, is not used for advertising or ad personalization, and is not used to track you across other companies' apps or websites. Advertising-ID collection by Analytics is turned off.

1.4 Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows banner ads to free (non-subscribed) users via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and approximate location (from IP address) to serve and measure ads.

  • If you decline App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS, your advertising identifier is not used, and non-personalized ads are shown instead.
  • See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

1.5 On-device text-to-speech

Word pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Text is processed on-device and is not sent to the Developer.

1.6 Sharing

When you share a word-age check result, the App creates the image on your device and hands it to your device's share sheet. Nothing is sent to the Developer.

2. In-app purchases

Premium subscriptions ("Yabai Word Premium") are processed entirely through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The Developer does not receive or store your payment information. Use "Restore Purchases" to restore your subscription on a device signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

3. Notifications

The App can send a local daily reminder notification at a time you choose. This notification is scheduled entirely on your device and does not involve any server.

4. Purpose of use

  • Displaying today's slang word and delivering the word database (Firebase)
  • Showing ads to free users (AdMob)
  • Providing in-app purchases (Apple / Google Play)
  • Understanding feature usage to improve the App (Firebase Analytics)
  • Sending local reminder notifications

The App is not directed at children under 13.
