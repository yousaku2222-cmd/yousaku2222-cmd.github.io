Yabai Word - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 14, 2026 / Last updated: September 14, 2026

"Yabai Word" (the "App") is provided by an independent developer, Yusaku Mizogami ("the Developer"). This policy explains what information the App handles.

1. Information the App collects and stores

1.1 Stored only on your device

The App does not require an account. The following information is stored only on your device and is never sent to the Developer:

  • Your favorited words and archive progress
  • Quiz history and your daily free-quiz usage status
  • Your notification time preference
  • Your premium subscription status (used to unlock features locally)

Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device (in-app purchase records are tied to your Apple ID and can be restored).

1.2 Content delivery (Firebase)

The App uses Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) solely to download the slang word database. No personal or account information is sent to Firebase.

1.3 Third-party services

The App shows banner ads to free (non-subscribed) users via Google AdMob. AdMob may collect the advertising identifier (IDFA), device information, and approximate location (from IP address) to serve and measure ads.

  • If you decline App Tracking Transparency permission on iOS, your advertising identifier is not used, and non-personalized ads are shown instead.
  • See Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) and how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services (https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

1.4 On-device text-to-speech

Word pronunciation uses your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. Text is processed on-device and is not sent to the Developer.

2. In-app purchases

Premium subscriptions ("Yabai Word Premium") are processed entirely through the Apple App Store. The Developer does not receive or store your payment information. Use "Restore Purchases" in Settings to restore your subscription on a device signed in with the same Apple ID.

3. Notifications

The App can send a local daily reminder notification at a time you choose. This notification is scheduled entirely on your device and does not involve any server.

4. Purpose of use

  • Displaying today's slang word and delivering the word database (Firebase)
  • Showing ads to free users (AdMob)
  • Providing in-app purchases (Apple)
  • Sending local reminder notifications

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13.

6. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated without prior notice. Changes take effect once posted on this page.
